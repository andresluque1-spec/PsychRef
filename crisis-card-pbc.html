Minors — know this cold before you need it

  • The Pavilion (St. Mary's Medical Center), 45th St corridor, West Palm Beach — the county's largest receiving facility accepting children.
  • County capacity for young children has been in flux since JFK North ended its under-13 Baker Act program (late 2024); NeuroBehavioral Hospitals has been standing up additional child beds, and a county comprehensive crisis facility is in development (groundbreaking targeted late 2026–early 2027). Verify current pediatric bed status at the moment of need via 211 or the receiving facility directly — this is the single most volatile row on this card.
  • Remember the 12-hour rule: receiving facilities must initiate a minor's examination within 12 hours of arrival, and law enforcement must give the guardian the facility information when transporting.

Non-emergency but urgent

  • Mobile response/crisis teams: access via 211 — Florida's Mobile Response Teams serve youth and young adults under 25 statewide; adult mobile crisis coverage in PBC routes through 211 and the managing entity (Southeast Florida Behavioral Health Network, sefbhn.org).
  • Ex parte route for families: Palm Beach County Clerk of Courts, mental health division — family petitions for involuntary examination; executed by PBSO. Useful when criteria exist but the patient isn't in front of you and law enforcement declined.
  • Marchman Act filings (substance-focused): same clerk pathway — pair with the Marchman page when built.
  • NAMI Palm Beach County — family education and support groups; the referral that helps the exhausted mother of your schizophrenia patient (namipbc.org).

The 60-second telehealth crisis drill (tie-in)

  1. Confirm the address on screen against check-in intake.
  2. Decide the lane: safety plan (SPI page) → voluntary ED with family transport → 911/CIT with Baker Act certificate (Baker Act page).
  3. Call with the script: "This is Andres Luque, psychiatric nurse practitioner with Psychiatry of the Palm Beaches. I'm initiating an involuntary examination under 394.463 for a patient currently at [address]. Requesting a CIT officer. Clinical basis: [two sentences]. Safety notes for responders: [weapons/agitation]."
  4. Fax/send CF-MH 3052b to the receiving facility, document times and badge numbers, notify supervising psychiatrist.
