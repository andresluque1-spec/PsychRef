Brief Therapy Toolkit

A focused intervention, one patient chosen practice step, and a printable worksheet for a 20–25 minute psychotherapy segment. English and Spanish.

A 20–25 minute shape

0–3 minSet an agenda and check immediate safety when indicated.
3–7 minChoose one concrete target and name what keeps it going.
7–18 minPractice one skill together using the patient's actual example.
18–25 minAgree on a small next step, anticipate a barrier, and recap.

Use clinical judgment. Acute risk, mania, psychosis, severe impairment, or trauma activation may require assessment, stabilization, or a different level of care before worksheet work. A brief segment is one part of care, not a complete course of psychotherapy.

Clinical basis and use

These are original, brief practice sheets adapted from established principles. They are prompts for individualized care, not diagnostic instruments or a substitute for a full protocol.

  • VA Provider's Guide to Brief CBT — agenda, skills practice, and concrete between-visit plans.
  • SAMHSA TIP 35 — motivational interviewing and patient directed change planning.
  • VA evidence brief — brief CBT and problem solving approaches in primary care.
